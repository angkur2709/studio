01 / PURPOSE

What we do

Theory inspires experiment.
Experiment challenges theory.

We build instruments, design experiments, and use X-rays to generate multiscale—from atomistic to continuum—and multimodal datasets of complex material behavior through full-field measurements.

These measurements fuel materials informatics, enable AI for materials design, and advance Physical AI for the next generation of experiments.
