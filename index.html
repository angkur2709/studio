What we do?

Theory inspires experiment. Experiment challenges theory.

We build instruments, design experiments, and use X-rays to generate multiscale (atomistic to continuum), multimodal datasets of complex material behavior through full-field measurements—fueling materials informatics to enable AI in materials design and Physical AI to design the next generation of experiments.

Research streams

Three ways of asking matter a question.

Studio brings together experimental mechanics, architected materials and data-driven discovery. Our research spans mechanical response, thermal transport and autonomous experimentation, linked by high-dimensional measurements of real materials.

Physical AI

Physical AI
for Mechanics

Can a machine autonomously interrogate a material until it understands its mechanical behavior? We create a closed loop that designs and prints specimens, characterizes their true geometry, measures full-field response, learns constitutive behavior, and chooses what experiment to perform next.

3D printing · XCT · DIC / DVC · X-ray diffraction · Constitutive AI

Mechanical metamaterials

Failure is
a design variable.

We investigate fracture, damage and failure in mechanical metamaterials using full-field experiments, theory and computation, including when Ashby-style scaling relations remain predictive and when architecture creates new mechanics.

Fracture · Failure · Scaling laws · Metallic lattices

Thermodynamic metamaterials

Structures as
thermal devices.

We explore architected materials not only as load-bearing solids but as thermodynamic devices, using topology, surface area and transport pathways to design heat exchangers and heat-spreading structures.

Heat exchangers · Entropy generation · Conduction · Convection

Current experiments

Ideas made physical.

Our current projects connect metamaterial design, local constitutive response and new experimental instrumentation.

Mechanical metamaterials

Ripstop gyroids

Interpenetrating gyroid networks create reinforced regions that can interrupt or arrest crack propagation, asking how topology can produce architected damage tolerance.

Thermodynamic metamaterials

Architected heat exchangers

Additively manufactured heat exchangers and heat-pipe concepts that use geometry to increase transport efficiency while reducing size and material use.

Local constitutive response

Stress–strain, everywhere

Optical full-field imaging and X-ray scattering reveal local elastic, plastic and total strain behavior in spatially heterogeneous materials.

Instrumentation

The Simmons Wagon

A custom elasto-plastic diffractometer combining stereo DIC and X-ray diffraction to separate elastic and plastic contributions to local deformation.
