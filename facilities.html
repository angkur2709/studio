Facilities

Build the instrument.
Then ask a new question.

Studio is developing an integrated experimental platform for manufacturing, X-ray characterization, full-field mechanics and local constitutive measurement.

X-ray beamline
3D + strain

Laboratory X-ray characterization

Microfocus X-ray imaging and scattering for tomography, local elastic-strain measurements and in-situ studies under mechanical load.

Full-field mechanics
DIC / DVC

Measure deformation as a field

Stereo digital image correlation and volumetric correlation connect boundary measurements to three-dimensional deformation inside materials.

Advanced manufacturing
MAKE

Specimens as controlled questions

Additive manufacturing enables control of architecture, porosity, orientation, defects and multimaterial layout so experiments can be designed rather than merely observed.

Simmons Wagon
σ(x), ε(x)

Local constitutive response

A custom elasto-plastic diffractometer combining stereo DIC and X-ray diffraction to separate total, elastic and plastic strain contributions.
